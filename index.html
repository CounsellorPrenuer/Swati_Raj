Welcome to Swapath

Choosing a stream, a subject, or a career path can feel overwhelming — for students and parents alike. At Swapath, I help students pause, reflect, and find their way forward, using structured assessments and honest, experience-backed guidance.

With over a decade in education — as a teacher, Vice-Principal, and curriculum developer across CBSE and Cambridge IGCSE systems — I understand not just careers, but how students actually learn, think, and grow.

Process

How It Works

A brief preview of the structured process to help you find your way.

1. Discovery Call

A short conversation to understand the student's situation and concerns.

2. Assessment

Aptitude, interest, and personality mapping using structured tools.

3. Guidance Session

A detailed discussion of options, strengths, and realistic paths forward.

4. Personalized Roadmap

A clear plan covering stream/subject choice, skill-building, and next steps.
